Key findings for 18–24 demographic

TikTok is the primary vector. 3,035 mentions with 29% positive sentiment — content is framed sympathetically, making it less likely to trigger moderation or viewer skepticism among young users.
Micro-accounts, macro-reach. ._af10x has only 397 followers yet achieved 1.9M reach — the hallmark of TikTok algorithmic amplification pushing content to undirected audiences.
Geopolitical framing as entry point. Users discover content through political solidarity channels, not explicit searches for antisemitic material.
X has the sharpest negative skew. 77% of X mentions are negative — a reactive/critical space, while TikTok functions as sympathetic. Different platforms, different emotional registers.
06

How content reaches the 18–24 cohort

STEP 01
Entry via solidarity
User engages with #freepalestine or #gaza content — framed as humanitarian or political solidarity, not ideological. No perceived red flags for a young viewer.
STEP 02
Algorithmic pull
TikTok/Bluesky algorithms serve adjacent content. #fyp and #viral tags are embedded in posts to maximize distribution to undirected viewers aged 18–24.
STEP 03
Normalization loop
Consistent exposure (17K mentions / 2 weeks, 75M reach) normalizes the framing. TikTok's 29% positive ratio means content is not perceived as hostile on first encounter.
STEP 04
Cross-platform spread
Content migrates to Bluesky (2,709), Reddit (496), and Telegram (82). Telegram links suggest flow into encrypted private channels beyond monitoring reach.
07

Data limitations

No direct age segmentation. Brand24 does not provide demographic breakdowns. The 18–24 inference is based on platform demographics (TikTok skews 18–29) and influencer account characteristics — not confirmed age data. True exposure is likely higher than reported.
This dataset tracks the keyword "antisemitism" — content that promotes antisemitic ideas without using the term (coded language, dog-whistles) would not appear here.
Instagram and Facebook show 0 mentions — almost certainly a Meta API access limitation, not a true absence of content on those platforms.
Telegram content is partially visible (82 mentions) but private channels are unmonitorable — this is the floor, not the ceiling, of Telegram exposure.
